# Known Limitations & Honest Blockers

1. **Multi-Source Human-Annotated Holdout Dataset (`BLOCKED`)**: Only one full-length real gaming source (`full-progressive.mp4`, 617.906 s GTA compilation) plus local short speech/synthetic fixtures exist locally. A 15–20 source cross-genre holdout set (racing, shooter, podcast, livestreams, no-highlight sources) with independent human annotators is NOT present locally; benchmark infrastructure supports it, and multi-genre coverage is tested via controlled synthetic/local fixtures while cross-source holdout Precision@3 remains honestly unverified until external authorized media & human labels are supplied.
2. **Human Editorial Approval**: Automated tests, VLM verification, and engineering storyboard inspection cannot substitute for final human operator approval. Outputs remain in `NEEDS_EDITORIAL_REVIEW` / `READY_FOR_PUBLISHING_REVIEW` until explicitly approved by a human reviewer.
3. **Consumer 4 GB VRAM Ceiling**: Concurrent Whisper + VLM + NVENC would exceed 4 GB VRAM. All heavy GPU workloads are strictly serialized via `HEAVY_LOCK`, bounded in image resolution/context length, unloaded (`keep_alive=0`) between stages, and have CPU/CV fallback paths.
4. **Optional Dependencies Not Bundled in Default Lean Image**: `transnetv2_pytorch`, `scenedetect`, and `rapidocr_onnxruntime` are not pinned in `requirements-factory.lock` to keep the Docker image lean and MIT-clean. Clean-room PyAV/OpenCV shot detection and graphic-card/text-band detection run by default, with optional adapters enabled automatically when those packages are installed.
5. **Monetization & Reused Content**: Technical editing, captions, hooks, or attribution do NOT grant copyright permission or YouTube monetization eligibility for third-party footage. Explicit operator rights confirmation and policy warnings are enforced.
